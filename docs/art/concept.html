Factory Heist · art concept

Thieves in the stacks, and a dispatcher with one searchlight.

Every shape on this page is hand-authored isometric geometry from sprites.js: no images, no CDN, no build. The board turns in 90-degree steps, the ceiling lamp does not turn with it, and the search - explored ground, held frontier, committed path - is painted onto the factory floor rather than replacing it.

The screen

One bay of the factory with the HUD mocked over it. The map is the screen: no panel, no boxes down the side, the verdict would arrive over the world. Use the rotate buttons - the art is the same models, turned.

All four rotations

The same bay, turned. Nothing is mirrored and nothing is re-drawn: the models rotate, the lamp stays in the top-left of the screen, so the face that was in the light hands over to its neighbour as the board turns.

Palette

Five colours carry the factory, and three more are reserved as signals. Nothing else reaches the canvas - tests/sprites.test.js fails the build if it does.

Search, painted on the floor

Left to right: bare plate, ground the search has taken, the frontier it is still holding, and the route it committed to. The frontier is lifted off the deck and lit safety yellow because it is literally the dispatcher's light; the path rides higher than everything, so no later tile can bury it.

The kit

Every sprite at every rotation that is a different picture. A piece that looks the same from all four sides says so rather than shipping the same drawing four times.

Silhouette test

The same kit as flat black at the size it is actually played at. If two pieces are confusable here they are confusable in the game: the three thieves have to differ in height and width, not in detail.
